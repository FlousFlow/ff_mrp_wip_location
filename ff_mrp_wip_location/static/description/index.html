Manufacturing WIP Location

Route selected manufactured products directly to a company-level Work in Progress location.

How it works

  1. Configure an active internal WIP location in Manufacturing Settings.
  2. Enable Under Processing on the products that need WIP routing.
  3. Confirm and complete the manufacturing order. The main finished product is stored in WIP.

Design principles

  • Opt-in per product; normal products keep standard Odoo destinations.
  • By-products keep native Odoo destinations.
  • No accounting or valuation entries are changed by this module.
  • Company-specific and shared internal locations are supported.

Compatibility: Odoo 20 Community, LGPL-3. Requires Manufacturing and Inventory.
